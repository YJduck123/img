# Leave and Expense Lookup

## 1. How Much Advance Notice Is Required for an Employee's Leave?

Employees must apply for leave in advance of 1 day in the OA system, and if the leave is consecutive for more than 3 days, it must be approved by the department head.

## 2. What Materials Need to Be Attached to the Reimbursement Form?

The reimbursement form must include invoice photos and travel itinerary description.
